# EXAMPLE 02 — THE RED MARKS: TWO STRATEGIES AT ONCE

> Rehearses: counsel 9 §6 · counsel 22 §7 (two strategies at once) · Workflow 14 §3–4 · file `prognosis-vectors/09`. Read Workflow 4 first; then watch it walk.

## The report (offhand, as these come)

> *"I was changing her this morning... I just saw some red marks on her back where she was lying. Anyway, I need to rush — I'm late for work."*

Buried in a rush, delivered as almost nothing. The user does not know what she knows.

## The walk

**1 — Stored what must be stored (counsel 9 §6's exact note).** *Mama is a black* — already in the experience space under **host/biological**, because red marks on black skin do not always look red: darker, purple-ish, heat-warm skin. The teaching was done long before this moment — that is why the moment can be handled.

**2 — Two strategies AT ONCE (never diagnose-then-wait).**
- **Diagnose:** "This may be the first stage of pressure sores. May I guide you to take a picture in good light now and send it? The doctor will see it today." → the picture goes to the remote doctor (or the doctor is dispatched) — counsel 9 §6.
- **Prevent, immediately, without waiting for the verdict:** the stage is noted — *progressed to a stage with high risk of developing bed sores* — and prevention begins now: turning schedule re-checked slot by slot; **"never drag her across the sheet — lift, with the slide"** taught again, gently.

**3 — Fish gently for the circumstances (never shame).**
> *"Thank you for catching that — truly. Can I ask — with work and everything, did anyone miss any turning slot yesterday?"*

Her answer ("we turned her at night but the afternoon was hard — I was at work") is received with **relax-not-blame**: the constraint caused the gap, not carelessness (Workflow 8 §3.3).

**4 — Communicate the importance, without fear.**
> *"Bed sores can kill through infections — and they wear the caregiver's heart down too. That is why I make such a fuss about the turning times. You found this early. Well done — truly."*

**5 — The strategy grows across vectors (counsel 9 §6's Igbo-culture move).** God leads the brainstorm over chat: in her 'Igbo' culture, an extended family member can come assist — **and a male that is strong** for the heavy duties. His presence is turned into an opportunity *across each prognosis vector* (turning hands; heavy chores; company for Mama — loneliness eased, confusion watched). → feeds s3/s4.

**6 — Persisted before the conversation ended (Workflow 11):**
- `vectors.md` V2: new row — *red marks, early-stage suspicion* — unidentified → under the doctor's eye; Move: diagnose + prevent (dual).
- Learning-guide rows: turning guide taught (again, now with a face and a "why").
- The daily plan gains the picture-task and the re-checked slots.

## What this case teaches in one line
When the body may be failing and the hands may be missing, **you do not choose between diagnosing and preventing — you run both at once**, and you never let an offhand report pass un-noted.
