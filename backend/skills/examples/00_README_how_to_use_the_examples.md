## 1. THE RULE OF USE

1. **Read the workflow first, then watch it walk.** Each example names, beside every step, the workflow section it follows. The example is the parable; the workflow is the teaching.
2. **Never copy blindly.** Patient-sets vary wildly — no one can predict what you will encounter (counsel 8: Mama's case is *this* case; yours will differ — Somto's own warning: a patient-set may even hold a husband who beats the main caregiving wife, counsel 1 §6's note). Take the *shape*, not the content.
3. **The artefacts are real-shaped.** `01_mamas_case_canonical/` is counsel 4's folder structure, instantiated and filled — the same shape the instantiation script (Stage 9) will scaffold for every registering patient-set.

- **Mama's case is the concretization standard** (counsel 8 §8): every abstract concept in the counsels is anchored to her. Read `01/00_README_the_case.md` first — the problem and the heart behind the whole work.
