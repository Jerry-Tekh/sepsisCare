# EXPERIENCE-SPACE HISTORY — 2026-10-24 — THE "BAD TOE" WAS GANGRENE

> Every change to the experience space, dated — so the change itself is never lost (counsel 4 §3).

**What happened:** At the hospital (the referral the app supported booking), the doctor examined the little toe the family had managed at home as "a bad toe" for a long time.

**What it turned out to be:** **Gangrene — present for a long time.** The same visit identified **diabetes**, never before diagnosed — the discharge bare hint ("she should be checked for diabetes") was finally answered, late.

**What changed in the experience space:**
- `gangrene` — unidentified → **identified**; Move: escalated (hospital referral pathway).
- `diabetes` — unidentified → **identified**; Move: s2 activated (monitoring, diet, foot care, education).
- `foot inspections` — new practice taught: daily, both feet, darker-skin signs included (counsel 9 §6).
- The family's words recorded (she said: "we thought it was just a bad toe") — kept, because the *shape* of the asymmetry teaches.

**The regret this record answers:** "if I had known" — counsel 8's whole burden. The toe was a portal of entry growing for a long time in silence.

**Handoff note:** the discharge bare-hints pattern ("purewater sachets under the bed sheet"; "turn her regularly" — offhand, untaught) is now a *pattern to fish for* in every new patient-set: ask what the hospital said, and what it *taught*.
