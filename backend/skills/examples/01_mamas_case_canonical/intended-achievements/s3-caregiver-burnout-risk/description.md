# S3 — CAREGIVER-BURNOUT-RISK — DESCRIPTION

> `intended-achievements/<id>-<issue_name>/description.md` — the issue and the goal (counsel 4 §3). Named by the ISSUE, not the solution.

## The issue (what is wrong)

The caregiver — Mama's daughter — is the almost-sole caregiver. She works 9–5. Her honest daily capacity for Mama is: Sun 20m, Mon 30m, Tue 1hr, Wed 2hrs, Thu 20m, Fri 40m, Sat 30m — and history shows she can execute tasks totalling **up to 30 minutes** before sleeping from tiredness. She has already sprained her waist carrying Mama. She carries guilt that drives her to overwork, then collapse. The care Mama needs (turning 2-hourly, dressing changes, sugar tests, meals, baths) cannot physically fit inside one pair of hands with thirty minutes a day.

**This is an issue of the Socio-Systemic vector sitting on the Caregiver entity — and it quietly throttles every other strategy (s1's turning schedule above all).**

## The intended-achievement (the goal that handles the issue)

By God's grace: the caregiver is **protected and sustained** — her workload carried by more hands than hers, her body guarded from injury, her soul carried from guilt to rest — so that Mama's care is fully executed *and* the caregiver does not break. ("Prevent caregiver breakdown" — counsel 5 §3's running strategy; counsel 1 §6: caregiver collapse leads directly to patient neglect.)

## The naming test (counsel 4 §3 — shown, not just stated)

*If the solution changes completely, does the folder name stay true?* Tomorrow the solution might be a rotation roster, a paid-and-verified helper, a relocated relative, or all three — the name `caregiver-burnout-risk` stays true through all of them. The name is right.
