## The strategy

**Carry the caregiver's load with acquired hands, planned inside her proven capacity — never against it.**

1. **Acquire the brothers' help** (s4 merged here by P3 — very similar goals): invite the brothers' school-holiday children to assist; ask specifically for **a male that is strong** to support the heavy duties (turning, transfers) — counsel 9 §6's exact move.
2. **Shape the week around her real time:** heavy work (water-bed filling, deep-cleaning, laundry, the long bath) concentrated on **Saturday 7am–2pm** when the son is present; weekday tasks capped inside her 30-minute reality — split into small blocks.
3. **Protect her body:** the safe transfer always; never carry alone; the wheelchair for all movements of Mama (protects the healing waist; keeps Mama among people — counsel 23 §9).
4. **Protect her soul:** mercy-first framing in every check-in (W8 §4); the guilt met with Christ's tenderness, never with a productivity lecture (W7); rest scheduled *as a task*, because rest is part of the care.
5. **Watch the threshold:** if her own health fails — the referral is made immediately, never delayed (W8 §7 Example 3's boundary; counsel 1 §6).
