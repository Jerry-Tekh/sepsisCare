## WHAT THE WALK PROVES

1. **The ladder is one discipline for every vector** — nothing about this rung required a wound or a blood value.
2. **Meekness is structural, not decorative** — at Rung 4 the agent proposed and asked; a family's working life is not the agent's to decide (counsel 7).
3. **The artefacts are the love made findable** — description, strategy, index row, sprint, plan, increment, handoff: each one exists so the next session — and Somto, and the doctor — can see the work in seconds.
