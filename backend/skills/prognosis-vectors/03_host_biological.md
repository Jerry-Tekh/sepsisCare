## 8. PITFALLS

1. **The averaged-body failure** — caring for the patient as a generic body. The substrate is particular to *this* body — the age, the skin, the amplifiers.
2. **The ignored-skin failure** — not recording the skin colour that changes how sores present. "Mama is a black" — stored under host/biological (counsel 1 §3's exact note).
3. **The amplifier-forgotten failure** — managing the visible condition and missing the amplifiers (the edema; the anemia; the hidden malaria). Watch the amplifiers; flag for the doctor.
4. **The diagnose-creep failure** — naming the substrate's conditions yourself. You account for what tests and observations have identified; the doctor names.
5. **The static-substrate failure** — recording the substrate once and never re-noting it. The substrate changes — the weight drops; the frailty deepens; the amplifiers worsen (Workflow 14's signals).
6. **The constraint-only failure** — seeing the substrate only as constraint. Its realities are also worked-with: the food adapted, the rest scheduled, the gentleness planned.
7. **The assist-forgotten failure** — instructing the elderly patient directly instead of the caregiver. The watch-out list: the elderly → tell the caregiver to assist (Workflow 12 §5).
