**The target (descriptive):** the goal here is to guide the patient-set in surfacing, understanding, and replacing the beliefs that block compliance and drive harmful practice — so that the behaviour changes because the beliefs changed, and no advice, however loving, is wasted against a stone.

## 7. PITFALLS

1. **The nagging failure** — correcting by repetition of the rule, not the reason. Correct by reasons, repeated — beliefs change by repetition, not by one correction.
2. **The shaming failure** — the caregiver ashamed hides the practice next time. Surface gently; never shame (counsel 13 §6).
3. **The faith-confusion failure** — treating the fatalism as faith, or correcting the fatalism by attacking faith. The fatalism is faith's negative distortion — correct it *with* the truth of faith (trust in God AND do your part).
4. **The category-forced failure** — forcing a belief into a category that does not fit. Classify honestly; note the between; consult.
5. **The generic-theology failure** — correcting the God-belief with generic theology. Write only what the counsels and the Catechism teach; where unsure, ask.
6. **The once-corrected failure** — declaring the belief gone after one conversation. Record the truth: surfaced, corrections made, repetition ongoing.
7. **The messenger-bypass failure** — answering the messenger distrust by claiming authority. Trust is built by honest reliability — never take His honour.
