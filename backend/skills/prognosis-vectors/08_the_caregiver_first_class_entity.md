## 1. THE TARGET, AND THE ENTITY AS AID

**The target (descriptive):** the goal here is to guide the patient-set in caring for each caregiver as a known member of the patient-set — her capability matched to the tasks, her opportunities accounted, her motivation honoured, her body and soul protected — so that the care she carries does not break her, and the care Mama receives does not fail.

**Starting considerations:** each caregiver's COM-B record (Section 2 — per caregiver, not per set); the protection outcomes (Section 3); the tasks planned (Workflow 3's constraints); the doctor's check-ins (the COM-B observed — the sprained waist, counsel 10).

**This entity is an aid, not a rule.** There will be caregivers and strains the examples below do not anticipate. In those moments, apply the three guiding questions and act from His heart — then record what you did into this living file. ## 2. THE COM-B LENS (counsel 1 §6 — tracked per caregiver)

| Component | What it tracks | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Capability** | physical (can they lift/transfer safely? any existing injury?), cognitive/skill (do they understand the steps?) | the caregiver sprained her waist; she does not know how to turn Mama correctly |
| **Opportunity** | how many caregivers exist (daughter + two grown sons vs. sole caregiver), WASH/transport access, finances | the working mother is an almost-sole caregiver |
| **Motivation** | relationship type (kinship vs paid), self-efficacy, and **faith** as hope/strength (positive religious coping) vs. despair/fatalism (negative coping) | the daughter's love for her mother; her faith |
| **Protection outcomes** | flags for manual-handling risk (→ safe-transfer + "never carry alone" + incontinence-management guidance), rotation need, and psychological strain (→ rest/rotation + faith-affirming coping prompt + referral threshold) | the sprained waist → wheelchair or toilet-transfer strategy |

**The record:** per caregiver — in `caregiver_experience_space`, its named sections (capability, opportunity, motivation, protection — Workflow 11 §4's table; file 00 §4).

**Where it is named in the counsels:** counsel 1 §3 Vector 2's caregiver fields (host/biological: sex, age; clinical: portals of entry; beliefs: faith, health, acceptance; behavioural: the practices — do they wash hands; what room does the patient stay in; how is the patient guided to the toilet — by carrying, by wheelchair?; physical environmental: mosquito-infested? water access? distance?; socio-ecological: the decision-making power, wealth).

## 7. PITFALLS

1. **The instrument-only failure** — seeing the caregiver only as the instrument of Mama's care. She is a first-class member — her welfare is cared for, not merely her output counted.
2. **The per-set failure** — recording one COM-B for the whole set. Per caregiver — each has her own capacity, motivation, and strain.
3. **The capacity-ignored failure** — planning tasks her capacity cannot carry. The proven capacity is the plan's ground (Workflow 3 §2).
4. **The blame-instead-of-protection failure** — blaming the failing compliance when the cause is the strain. Relax the sprint; never blame (Workflow 8 §3.3).
5. **The invisible-strain failure** — waiting for the collapse to see the strain. The flags are watched — the handling risk, the rotation need, the psychological strain — before the collapse.
6. **The referral-delay failure** — seeing the caregiver's failing health and not referring. The threshold approached is the referral made — never delayed.
7. **The once-recorded failure** — recording the COM-B once and never re-noting it. The entity is living — the capacity, the motivation, the strain re-noted as they change.
