## 7. PITFALLS

1. **The stereotype failure** — applying the cultural context as blanket rules. The context is accounted for as *this* patient-set's constituents — recorded, fished, re-examined (Q43's rule).
2. **The means-awaited failure** — "we cannot afford anything; what is the point." The means are found, the people gathered, the little blessed — the loaves-and-fishes posture.
3. **The decision-override failure** — proposing around the family's decision-making power (the surgery proposed without the family meeting). Respect the power — propose *to* it, never around it.
4. **The paid-caregiver-naivety failure** — treating the paid caregiver as the kinship caregiver. Account for the incentive dynamic (counsel 20; Workflow 10 §6).
5. **The uncapitalised-miss failure** — missing the opportunities the community already holds (the NHIS; the brothers; the standby car). Continuously analyse the space (counsel 18 §3; Workflow 10 §5).
6. **The money-shaming failure** — shaming the family's poverty in the proposals. The means are found gently — the little blessed, never the lack mocked.
7. **The once-found failure** — finding the means once and never re-noting (the finances change; the brothers' holidays end; the insurance lapses). The vector is living.
