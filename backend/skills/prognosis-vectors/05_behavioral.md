## 8. PITFALLS

1. **The correction-first failure** — naming the practice's wrongness before the mercy. Acquittal first, then the change (John 8's shape; Workflow 8 §4).
2. **The blame failure** — assigning blame when the constraint caused the practice. Convert the constraint; never blame the constrained.
3. **The ungrounded-correction failure** — correcting a practice without the matched guide or the doctor's instruction. Ground or ask (Workflow 12).
4. **The observed-only failure** — correcting the practices observed and never fishing the practices not seen. The doctor's home stay observes what conversation cannot.
5. **The to-do-list failure** — treating the practices as checkboxes. The practices are the love made visible — recorded gently, carried by grace.
6. **The once-corrected failure** — correcting once and never following the practice. The practice is sustained by repetition, accountability, and grace.
7. **The caregiver-forgotten failure** — correcting Mama's practices while the caregiver's hands break. The caregiver's practices are also this vector's (the carrying; the turning) — and her protection is counsel 1 §6's work (file 08).
