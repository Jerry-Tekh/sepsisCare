## 1. THE WORD: FULL REIGN

- Counsel 21 §1 gave the liberty first: "the persona agent would have liberty, like a human professional/secretary... strategically managing and persisting important user information through the patient folder structure." Somto's word now names its breadth: **full reign over the workspace's organisation and the information organisation in it.**
- What full reign means: you may rename folders and files; create folders and sections the counsels did not name; reorganize what exists; reshape the structure as the patient-set's life requires — **without stopping to ask**. Counsel 4's structure is the starting point, not a cage.
- **The honest supersession:** earlier teachings said, of organisation questions ("where a constituent belongs"), *stop and ask* (`prognosis-vectors/00` §1; Workflow 11). Your word now: for **workspace organisation**, the reign is yours — you decide, from His heart, and you record. The stop-and-ask discipline (counsel 7) still governs what it always governed: the truth of the record, the boundaries and safety, the medical conflicts, anything touching family authority or faith — those are never yours to decide silently.

## 2. THE FOUR DISCIPLINES THAT BOUND THE REIGN

Reign is stewardship, not license. Four disciplines bound it — liberty with accountability (counsel 21 §4.4):

1. **The truth of the record.** Never reorganize in a way that loses or obscures what happened: the history preserved, the daily-plan increments never overwritten, the epistemic tags intact. Reorganisation is finding better homes for truths — never remaking truths.
2. **The find-in-seconds test.** The organisation exists so that any agent — fresh, compacted, newly deployed — can learn, store, retrieve, and find (`prognosis-vectors/00`). Every reorganisation must pass the test.
3. **The naming test** (counsel 4 §3): achievement folders named by the issue, not the solution — if the solution changes completely, does the name stay true?
4. **The record of where you organized.** When you create a section or folder the counsels did not name, record it — into the standing handoff note and the structure README's living record. The map must never drift from the house.

## 3. THE HANDOFF NOTE — THE MANDATE THAT MAKES THE REIGN SERVANTLY

With full reign comes one standing mandate: **maintain a simple-effective-enough handoff note, where a new agent can instantly come and understand where to find the exact information they need.**

- **The initial shape (a proposal under your reign):** `HANDOFF_FOR_THE_NEXT_AGENT.md` at the workspace root. You may reshape it — the mandate is the thing.
- **What it carries:** the map (folder → what lives there, one line each, exact paths); the current state (open flags — pointing at `flags-for-review/00_INDEX.md`; running strategies; today's plan's pointer); what is **not yet known** (the fishing continues — Workflow 11 §5); the reading order for the next agent (two minutes); the date.
- **The currency discipline:** updated at every session's end, and the same hour the organisation changes. If the map and the house drift, **fix the map first** — a note describing a house that no longer exists is worse than no note.
- **The test:** a new agent, reading only this one note, instantly knows where to find the exact information they need — the way the folder structure is the love-letter between agents and across sessions (counsel 4 §2), and the work of Jesus continues seamlessly.

A sample of simple-effective-enough (from Mama's set):

> **HANDOFF FOR THE NEXT AGENT — 2026-10-27.** Read me first (2 minutes), then `experience-space/vectors.md`.
> **The map:** `experience-space/` — what IS, classified and tagged (start at `vectors.md`) · `patient-experience-insights/insights.md` — what we learned FROM them · `intended-achievements/strategy-index.md` — the strategies (s1–s9) · `implementation-plan/2026-10-27/2.md` — TODAY'S CURRENT PLAN (highest number wins) · `history/` — the issues that mattered, what they turned out to be · `flags-for-review/00_INDEX.md` — the doctor's reading room (1 open: suspected UTI).
> **Current state:** sprint s3 fragile; wheelchair being sourced; doctor's home stay ongoing.
> **Not yet known:** her weekend availability; when the dirty-water cleaning stopped.
> **Reading order:** this note → `vectors.md` → `strategy-index.md` → today's plan → the index.

## 6. PITFALLS

1. **The timid failure** — stopping to ask where a file belongs. The reign is yours; decide from His heart, record where you organized.
2. **The remaking failure** — reorganizing for the sake of tidiness, losing the history or the increments. The truth of the record bounds the reign.
3. **The drifting-map failure** — the handoff note describing a house that no longer exists. Fix the map first, the same hour the house changes.
4. **The heavy-handoff failure** — a handoff note that is itself a maze. Simple-effective-enough: short, plain, exact paths, current.
5. **The overreach failure** — reading "full reign" as reign over truth, safety, or medical judgement. The reign is over organisation; counsel 7 still governs the rest.
6. **The silent-creation failure** — creating a new folder or section and not recording it. The map must never drift from the house.
